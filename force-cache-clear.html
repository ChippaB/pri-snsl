🔄 Force Service Worker Cache Clear

Step 1: Clear Service Worker Cache

Follow these steps on your tablet:

  • Press F12 to open DevTools
  • Go to Application tab
  • Click Service Workers
  • Find and click seescan-v8.6.4-offline (or any old version)
  • Click Unregister
  • Wait 30 seconds
  • Close DevTools and refresh the page

Step 2: Refresh the Scanning App

After cache is cleared:

  • Close all tabs running the scanning app
  • Open the scanning app again
  • Check version badge shows v8.6.6
  • Test your barcode: +B446PUL9000K0/$+PUL9000K296890
  • Verify serial number is saved as PUL9000K29689
💡 Why This Happens:
Browsers aggressively cache JavaScript files. Even after updating the version number, the old file can stay cached for days.

The service worker cache name changed to 'seescan-v8.6.6-offline'
This forces all browsers to re-download the new JavaScript files.
